BQ-RELIABILITY Juanes

System Reliability — What percentage of plate readings require admin review due to low OCR confidence?

Every parking record stores an ocrConfidence score (0–1). Readings below 0.75 are flagged for manual admin review. Thanks to eventual connectivity, entries captured offline in the basement (no signal) are still saved with their OCR score and synced back to Firestore automatically — so this reliability view is always complete, even after a connectivity gap.

How Real-Time Sync Works
  • iOS: Firestore addSnapshotListener on parkingSpots fires on every document write — no polling, sub-second latency.
  • State transitions written to vehicleRecords with plate, floor, spot, and timestamp on every entry and exit event.
  • Validation method: occupied count from parkingSpots (isAvailable=false) is cross-checked against the net event-log delta. Drift indicates missed events or stale writes.
  • Queue: config/parking.queueLength is updated live by the admin app; estimated wait = queueLength × 5 min avg service time.
